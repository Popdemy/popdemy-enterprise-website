About Popdemy Enterprise

African Coffee. Global Experience.

Popdemy Enterprise is an artisan coffee business built around a straightforward model: sourcing coffee from Ugandan producers, roasting and packaging selected products under the Popdemy Coffee brand, collaborating with trusted suppliers, and serving customers directly.

Our ambition is to bridge local Ugandan coffee craftsmanship with coffee enthusiasts across Africa and around the world.
